Retained hand source and tools

Original: Emma L. D. Lieker, anatomical-hand-model, CC BY-NC 4.0.
The unchanged source LEFT hand, original licence and attribution remain in inputs/.
AdultRightHand.blend is the retained calibrated neutral right-hand rig; calibration.json
and articulation_check.json document its 180 mm hand length, 78 mm palm breadth and
articulation check. AdultRightHand.png is its neutral preview.

build_study.py and check_contact.py retain the reusable hand preparation and contact
methods. They use the original handle source and the installed modelling runtime.
Old posed grip scenes, invalid redesigned-handle studies, diagnostic renders and logs
were removed on 2026-10-28. A generic hand rig does not establish user comfort or fit.
